Topic: Wildfire Analysis and Remote Sensing

Goal: Analyze satellite remote sensing data from multiple sensors to characterize fire extent, soil burn severity, and surface deformation from a recent wildfire event.

Datasets:

  • Location: /home/jovyan/shared/NASA_Summer_School_2026/wildfires/materials/Data
  • Eaton Fire BAER Soil Burn Severity - Burn severity data from post-fire analysis
  • Eaton Fire NIFC Perimeter - Fire perimeter from National Interagency Fire Center
  • Eaton Fire UAVSAR - Synthetic aperture radar data characterizing surface deformation
  • Background and Resources - Supporting reference materials and documentation

Scripts and Notebooks:

  • Location: /home/jovyan/shared/NASA_Summer_School_2026/wildfires/materials
  • Jupyter notebooks for data analysis and visualization
  • Tools for working with SAR, optical, and burn severity data

Geographic Focus: Eaton Fire event (Southern California)

Questions:

  1. How do satellite sensors detect and map wildfire extent and boundaries?
  2. What is the relationship between different measures of burn severity?
  3. How can synthetic aperture radar (SAR) be used to detect land surface deformation caused by wildfires?
  4. How do fire characteristics vary spatially across the fire perimeter?
  5. What are the advantages and limitations of different remote sensing approaches for wildfire analysis?
