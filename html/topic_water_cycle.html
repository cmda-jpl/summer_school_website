Topic: Human Influences on the Water Cycle

Goal: Analyze satellite observations of terrestrial water storage changes to investigate human and climate influences on regional water cycles, with a focus on groundwater depletion and drought impacts.

Datasets:

  • Location: /home/jovyan/shared/NASA_Summer_School_2026/water_cycle/materials/data
  • GRACE/GRACE-FO Total Water Storage (TWS) data - Satellite gravimetry measurements of water mass changes
  • Gridded data: GRCTellus.JPL.200204_202603.GLO.RL06.3M.MSCNv04CRI.nc
  • Regional Analysis for California (CA):
    • GRACEFO_TimeSeries_CA_2002_2026.csv - Time series of water storage anomalies
    • GRACEFO_RegCoef_CA.csv - Regional regression coefficients
    • GRACEFO_RegResid_CA.csv - Regional residuals

Scripts and Notebooks:

  • Location: /home/jovyan/shared/NASA_Summer_School_2026/water_cycle/materials
  • GRACE_Extract_TS.ipynb - Extract time series from GRACE data
  • GRACE_Map_TWS.ipynb - Visualize water storage changes spatially
  • GRACE_NB1_GPTrend_Example.ipynb - Gaussian Process regression for trend analysis
  • GRACE_NB2_GPFit_torch.ipynb - Fit Gaussian Process models to water cycle data
  • GRACE_NB3_GPPredict_torch.ipynb - Make predictions using GP models
  • GRACE_NB4_Bootstrap_torch.ipynb - Uncertainty quantification via bootstrap methods
  • Supporting files: h2oenv (environment), ExpCov_SimField.png (example output)

Geographic Focus: California and global water storage patterns

Questions:

  1. How have terrestrial water storage changes varied over time, and what do these changes reveal about groundwater depletion and drought?
  2. Can satellite gravimetry data be used to detect anthropogenic water use (e.g., irrigation, groundwater pumping) at regional scales?
  3. How do human influences on water storage compare with natural climate variability?
  4. What are the trends and seasonal cycles in regional water storage, and how have they changed over the satellite observation period?
  5. How can uncertainty quantification methods improve our understanding of water cycle changes?
